Delivery

Where we deliver, and what it costs

We deliver from the kitchen on Murrays Gate Road, Alwarpet. Riders are ours or from Borzo, so the food goes straight to you and does not sit at a hub.

AREADISTANCETYPICAL DELIVERY
Mylapore, Alwarpet, RoyapettahUp to 3 km15–25 min ride
Teynampet, Nandanam, R.A. Puram3 to 5 km20–30 min ride
Adyar, T. Nagar, Egmore5 to 7 km30–40 min ride
Delivery is charged at whatever the rider costs for your distance — we do not add anything on top, and you see the exact figure before you pay. There is no minimum order.
How long it takes

Thirty to sixty minutes, depending on the dish and how busy the kitchen is. Thalis and parathas are cooked after you order, not held.

Ordering ahead

Tell us a time and the food leaves the kitchen for it. Lunch and dinner get busy, so an hour's notice means it reaches you hot.

Outside 7 km

We cannot deliver that far and keep the food hot. Pickup from Alwarpet works, and catering orders travel further by arrangement.

Paying

UPI when you order, or cash to the rider. The total includes the delivery charge, so nothing is added at the door.
