Our story

Kavita Lath, cooking in Mylapore since 2015

Tulsi Foods began with home delivery to a handful of families who wanted vegetarian food that tasted like their own kitchen rather than a restaurant.

The recipes are Kavita's, from her family in Rajasthan — dal with a proper tadka, sabzi that changes with what the market has, phulka rolled to order.

It is a pure vegetarian kitchen: no eggs, and no-onion-garlic or Jain preparations cooked separately when you ask.
