How to order from Tulsi Foods

You can order on this website or on WhatsApp. Either way the order goes straight to our kitchen in Alwarpet. There's no app to install and no account to create, and you pay exactly what the checkout shows.

On the website On WhatsApp Paying Ordering ahead After you order Delivery fees

Order on the website

  1. Open the menu. Search for a dish, or tap a section like Thalis or Parathas.
  2. Tap + to add dishes. Most mains come as a full or half portion. Your cart stays saved if you leave the page.
  3. Tap Checkout. Enter your phone number and name. If you've ordered before, your saved address fills in.
  4. Choose delivery or pickup, and when. As soon as possible, or a lunch (12:00 to 2:30 PM) or dinner (6:30 to 9:30 PM) window on any day in the next two weeks.
  5. For delivery, set your address. Pick it from the suggestions, then drag the pin onto your door and add a landmark. The pin decides your delivery fee, so please place it carefully.
  6. Choose UPI or cash, and place the order. You'll get a WhatsApp message from us straight away with your order number and a tracking link.

Order on WhatsApp

  1. Send "hi" to {{ wa_order_display }}. That's our ordering number. You can also tap the WhatsApp link on this site.
  2. Tap Order now. It opens our menu with your number already filled in, so checkout is quicker.
  3. Or stay in WhatsApp. Tap Order in WhatsApp to browse by section, or just type a dish name like "paneer" or "chaat". Add dishes and send the cart.
  4. Fill in the short checkout form. Delivery or pickup, address, time and payment, all on one screen. Check the summary and tap Place order.

Got a question instead? Type it in the same chat and it goes to the kitchen. They usually reply within a few minutes.

Paying

  • UPI: after you place the order, tap Pay. It opens GPay, PhonePe or Paytm with the amount filled in. You can also scan the QR code from another phone. The kitchen confirms your payment on WhatsApp.
  • Cash: pay the rider, or pay at the counter if you're picking up.
  • Your total already includes packing (₹{{ packing_fee }}, or ₹{{ packing_fee_large }} on orders over ₹{{ "{:,}".format(packing_threshold) }}), 5% GST and the delivery fee. Nothing is added later.

Ordering ahead

  • Pick the lunch or dinner window and a date up to 14 days ahead. The food is cooked for that window, not reheated.
  • We're open {{ hours_text }}. Outside those hours you can still order ahead for the next window.
  • On Mondays we take orders from 2 PM, so choose dinner.

After you order

  • Updates come on WhatsApp when the kitchen accepts your order, when it's ready and when the rider leaves, with a live rider link.
  • Track it any time from the link in those messages, or on the track page.
  • Wrong address? On the website you have 3 minutes after ordering to fix it. After that, call us.
  • Need to cancel? Call {{ store_phone }} before we start cooking. If you've already paid by UPI, we refund it in full. See the refund policy.
  • Picking up? Collect it at {{ pickup }}. It's usually ready in about 20 minutes.
